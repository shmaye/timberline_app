Layout

  • Departments down the left; they collapse to icons, and on a phone they slide in from the menu button.
  • Each department's tabs run across the top of the page. No dashboard of tiles.
  • One top bar everywhere: search, EN/FR, notifications, Assistant, you.
  • The Assistant docks on the right on wide screens and opens over the page on small ones.
  • Every record shows where it stands (Needs sheet › Proposal › … › Invoiced).

Colour rules

  • Dark rail and quiet grey ground, so numbers and wood stand out.
  • One brand colour for buttons, links and the selected tab.
  • The bright crayon colour marks only "you are here" and the Assistant.
  • Green, amber and red mean only OK, close to a limit, and over. Bundles are drawn in wood tones.
  • Light and dark mode both work; it follows the computer's setting.

Type

  • Archivo for headings: sturdy and industrial.
  • Inter for everything you read.
  • A fixed-width font for board feet, weights and money, so the columns line up.
