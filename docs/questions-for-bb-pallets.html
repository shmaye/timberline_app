As we set up Timberline, here are our questions, grouped by topic.

A. Documents we'd like to see

  1. Can you send a real customer "needs" spreadsheet, and the load worksheet you rework it in (the one showing % of a truck)?
  2. Can you send a recent inventory count sheet and your month-end calculation?
  3. Can you send a price list for one customer, and the cost sheet behind it?
  4. Can you send your catalog of items, in whatever form it exists today?
  5. Can you send a bill of lading, packing slip, invoice and subcontractor job sheet as they look today?

B. Loads and trucks

  1. What limits a load in practice: board feet (about 44,000), weight, or floor space? Does it change by trailer type?
  2. Which trailer types do you use (53' dry van, 48' flatbed, tri-axle, B-train, others)? What are their length, width, height, axles and weight limits?
  3. How are bundles placed on the truck: rows across, how high they stack, and any rules (heavy at the bottom, sizes kept together)?
  4. Do you deliver to more than one plant on one truck?
  5. How are filler items chosen, and how does the real loaded quantity come back to you?
  6. When a load is split in two, is the second load its own order?

C. Pricing

  1. Which lumber cost goes into a price: today's market, the average of loads bought, or a round planning number?
  2. What is the $700 "additional cost" on that customer? Do other customers have costs like it?
  3. How often are prices updated, and what triggers an update?
  4. Is freight set per destination? Who pays it, delivered vs pickup?
  5. Do you quote per piece, per bundle, per MBF, or per load?

D. Buying and inventory

  1. Is the "10-foot count" your standard way of comparing lumber prices?
  2. Where do offcuts go, and are they tracked?
  3. Where is inventory physically kept: your own yard, each subcontractor's yard, or both?
  4. Is inventory counted by item/size/length, or as total board feet?
  5. How do you keep the subcontractor's own wood separate from yours?

E. Subcontractors

  1. How many subcontractors do you use, and does each do different work (cutting, grooving, pallets)?
  2. How is each one paid: per MBF in, per piece, per load? Plus extras?
  3. How does a subcontractor tell you what was cut and loaded?

F. Money and systems

  1. What program are orders, invoices and inventory kept in today?
  2. Which accounting software (QuickBooks, Sage, other) must invoices and payments go into?
  3. What are your payment terms and credit limits, and who follows up on late payers?
  4. Which customers are billed in USD and which in CAD?
  5. Do you have any Quebec customers (QST, French documents)?
  6. Do you use a customs broker for US loads? Which papers go with the truck?

G. People and access

  1. Who will use the system (owner, office, buyer, subcontractors, drivers)?
  2. Who may see costs and margins, and who must not?
  3. Will anyone use it on a phone or tablet (counts at the yard, on the road)?
  4. Which email do you use (Outlook/Hotmail, Gmail, other)?

Thank you,
