Loading order

First on goes to the nose. Use the arrows to change the order.
Example figures. Real bundle sizes, pieces and weights will come from the catalog. Wood weight: SPF dry 1.90 ± 0.20, hemlock green 3.00 ± 0.30, hardwood pallets 4.00 ± 0.50 lb per board foot. The weight gauge shows the light side, average and heavy side; choose which to plan on. Bundles are packed tight: no space between them unless you set a gap between rows. Each block is one bundle, outlined so you can tell them apart. Extra layers (less than a full bundle) go on top of the last bundle and make it taller. Risers (3½" 4x4 dunnage) sit under every bundle and count in the height. The gaps between them (the fork openings) always face where the forklift unloads: the sides on a flatbed, the back in a van. When a bundle goes across, its risers are turned too, so the job sheet must tell the subcontractor which way to build it. Pallets need no risers.
